Paphia — Terms and purchases

Paphia uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. The following explains the app's purchase options; it does not replace that agreement or limit rights provided by applicable law.

Paphia Pro

Three plants have complete core use for free. A verified monthly or annual Pro subscription unlocks additional plant capacity and creation of complete portable collection archives while access is active. Lifetime is a one-time purchase that permanently unlocks those local capabilities. It does not promise future paid cloud or AI services. Optional support purchases do not unlock features and are not subscriptions.

Prices and billing periods are shown in the app using the current App Store product information. Annual subscriptions charge the annual total, not the displayed monthly equivalent. Savings compare the annual total with twelve monthly payments in the same currency; they are not a free trial.

Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled through your Apple Account's subscription controls. Deleting Paphia, turning off iCloud, or purchasing Lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription. If you move from a subscription to Lifetime, review and cancel the subscription separately to avoid future renewal charges. Manage an Apple subscription.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for your original purchase to restore eligible subscription or Lifetime access. Consumable support purchases do not create a restorable membership entitlement. Your App Store account can differ from your iCloud account.

If a subscription expires or a purchase is revoked, existing collection records remain accessible. You can continue editing existing records and adding observations; capacity restrictions apply to new plants and paid access is required for new complete archive creation. Imports and recovery of existing valid archives remain available.

Apple processes payments and refund requests. Request a refund from Apple. Support: paphia@sent.com.
