Paphia Privacy Policy

Paphia is an independent orchid collection app. For privacy questions, contact paphia@sent.com.

Your collection

Your plant records, observations, collection organization and original photos are stored in the app's local library. You do not need a separate Paphia account. Paphia does not include advertising, tracking SDKs or a developer-operated analytics service.

If you choose to enable iCloud synchronization, collection data and photos are sent to Apple's CloudKit service in your private iCloud database, associated with your Apple Account. Synchronization requires your choice and an available iCloud account. Your collection is not published to other Paphia users. Apple operates iCloud under its own privacy policy and terms.

Photos you import may contain embedded metadata, including location or camera information. Paphia preserves original photo files. That metadata can therefore remain in local files, synchronized originals and portable archives you export. Review files before sharing them with others.

Purchases

Apple handles payments, subscriptions and purchase restoration. Paphia uses StoreKit transaction and subscription information to determine which features you can use. We do not receive your payment card details. Canceling a subscription or losing a paid entitlement does not erase your existing collection. Apple handles refund requests and payment processing.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. Send only the information needed for support; a complete collection or original photos are not required unless you choose to provide them. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Your choices

You can use a local library, manage synchronization through the app's iCloud settings, edit or remove collection records, and export portable archives. Turning off synchronization does not itself delete copies already stored in iCloud. Device backups, copies on other devices, exported files and email attachments are separate copies that may need to be managed separately. Removing the app is not a substitute for deleting every external copy.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The published page will identify its revision date. Questions and privacy requests: paphia@sent.com.

Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Paphia 隐私政策

Paphia 是一款独立开发的兰花馆藏应用。隐私问题请联系 paphia@sent.com。

你的植物记录、观察、馆藏整理信息和原始照片保存在应用的本地资料库中。你无需注册单独的 Paphia 账号。Paphia 不包含广告、跟踪 SDK 或由开发者运营的分析服务。

如果你选择开启 iCloud 同步，馆藏数据和照片会传输至 Apple 的 CloudKit 服务，保存在与你的 Apple 账户关联的私有 iCloud 数据库中。同步需要你的选择和可用的 iCloud 账号，不会将馆藏公开给其他 Paphia 用户。Apple 按其自身隐私政策和条款运营 iCloud。

导入的照片可能含有位置、相机等嵌入式元数据。Paphia 保留原始照片，因此这些信息可能继续存在于本地文件、同步原片和导出的便携档案中。与他人分享前，请检查文件内容。

付款、订阅和购买恢复由 Apple 处理。Paphia 使用 StoreKit 提供的交易和订阅信息判断可用权益；开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息。取消订阅或失去付费权益不会删除现有馆藏。退款和支付问题由 Apple 的相应流程处理。

如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到邮件地址、正文及你主动提供的附件，并将其用于回复和调查问题。请仅提供解决问题所需的信息；除非你主动选择提供，否则无需发送整个馆藏或原始照片。你可以联系我们请求删除支持邮件，但依法需要保留的信息除外。

你可以使用本地资料库、在应用的 iCloud 设置中管理同步、编辑或移除记录，并导出便携档案。关闭同步本身不会删除已经存储在 iCloud 中的副本。设备备份、其他设备上的副本、导出文件和邮件附件需要分别管理；卸载应用不代表所有外部副本均已删除。

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本政策，并在公开页面标明修订日期。联系邮箱：paphia@sent.com。
